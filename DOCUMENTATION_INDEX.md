## 🎯 Key Concepts

### B2C (Business-to-Consumer)
- Individual customers
- Standard pricing
- Simple checkout
- Consumer payment methods

### B2B (Business-to-Business)
- Business accounts
- Volume pricing
- Purchase orders
- Credit terms
- Invoicing

### Retailer/Marketplace
- Multiple vendors
- Commission model
- Vendor dashboard
- Multi-vendor checkout
